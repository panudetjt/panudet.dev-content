## Three stages, one idea

A zettel is small enough to finish in one sitting. The labels are about how far the idea has travelled:

1. **Fleeting** — captured, not processed. Allowed to be messy.
2. **Literature** — tied to a source. The source is part of the note.
3. **Permanent** — rewritten in my own words and linked to something else.

The point is not to file everything. The point is to notice when an idea is ready to move.

## A note that never moves is still useful

Some fleeting notes stay fleeting forever. That is not a failure; it is a signal that the idea did not earn more attention. A garden is allowed to have annuals.

> The value of a note is not its length. It is the number of future thoughts it makes possible.
